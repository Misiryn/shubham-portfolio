01 Problem Discovery: The 95% Cold InMail Failure Rate

LinkedIn is the world's largest professional network, yet professional mentorship on the platform is fundamentally broken. Aspiring product managers, engineers, and career switchers send thousands of cold InMails weekly asking senior leaders to "pick their brain" or "connect for a 30-minute coffee chat."

Our discovery audit revealed that over 95% of these cold outreach messages are either ignored or declined. Both sides experience acute mutual frustration:

  • Seekers feel invisible, demoralized, and assume leaders simply don't care.
  • Mentors feel overwhelmed by vague, unbounded requests that threaten their limited calendar availability.

02 User Research: 25+ Semi-Structured Interviews

I conducted 25+ in-depth user interviews across two cohorts: 13 senior leaders (Directors, VPs, Group PMs) and 14 career aspirants.

The pivotal discovery was counter-intuitive: Senior leaders actually WANT to help junior talent. However, they fear open-ended commitments:

"When someone asks for '30 minutes to chat', I know it will turn into 45 minutes of unfocused questions where they haven't prepared. If they told me: 'I have 2 specific resume questions and need 15 minutes max', I would say yes 8 times out of 10."

03 Product Architecture: The Micro-Sprint Paradigm

I formulated the "Micro-Mentorship Sprint" framework governed by three structural constraints:

1. 15-Minute Hard Stop

Strictly enforced calendar ceiling removing the anxiety of a runaway meeting.

2. Mandatory Agenda Selection

Mentee must select 1 of 3 pre-defined structured agendas (Resume, Mock Interview, Career Pivot).

3. 1-Tap Calendar Booking

Zero back-and-forth email scheduling: mentors publish available sprint blocks directly.

04 High-Fidelity Prototyping & Design Iteration in Figma

Built a full mobile prototype in Figma. Initial user testing revealed a major friction point: users felt overwhelmed by standard multi-month calendar pickers when selecting a time slot.

I iterated the UI into one-tap immediate availability chips (e.g. "Today 6:00 PM", "Tomorrow 12:30 PM"). This single UX refinement reduced booking completion time by 45%.

05 Mutual Accountability & No-Show Prevention

A critical failure mode in free mentorship is no-shows. We introduced a Mutual Reliability Score:

  • Mentees receive an automated 2-hour pre-sprint SMS confirmation requiring a 1-tap confirmation.
  • Unexcused no-shows trigger a 30-day booking cooldown, protecting mentors' time.

06 Usability Testing Outcomes & Validation

80% Unassisted Task Completion

Tested with 10 first-time users; 8 completed the full discovery-to-booking journey without asking a single clarifying question.

9/10 Mentors Approved

9 out of 10 interviewed leaders stated they would actively participate if requests were constrained to 15 minutes with pre-set agendas.

07 PM Retrospective: Constraints Create Trust

This project demonstrated that the most effective UX solution is often the introduction of healthy constraints. By eliminating ambiguity and bounding time commitment, you convert passive goodwill into active, high-trust human connections.
