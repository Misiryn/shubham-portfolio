01 Problem Discovery: Enterprise Catalog Ingestion Chaos

As Reliance Retail scaled its omni-channel presence, onboarding enterprise giants like Netmeds (online pharmacy) and JioMart into Catalogue Cloud became a critical operational bottleneck.

Pharmaceutical catalogs consist of hundreds of thousands of complex items: medicines vary across dosages (250mg, 500mg, 650mg), packaging types (strip, bottle, blister pack), and regulatory tax schedules. Existing systems treated every SKU as an isolated flat record. When bulk CSV files were uploaded, minor column naming differences or missing mandatory drug fields caused batch uploads to fail silently or corrupt live catalog listings.

02 Catalog Operations & Category Manager Pain Points

I partnered with catalog operations teams to trace why onboarding a single enterprise brand was taking weeks. The root causes were clear:

  • No Parent-Child Hierarchy: Changing a brand description or contraindication warning required updating 40 individual variant rows manually.
  • Opaque Error Reporting: When a 50,000-row file failed, the server returned generic error messages like "Upload failed at line 1402: Type mismatch", forcing managers to comb through raw spreadsheets.

03 Product Architecture: Hierarchical SKU Variant Engine

I authored the comprehensive PRDs defining the Parent-Child Variant Architecture:

Parent Master SKU: Holds brand identity, core category taxonomy, regulatory medical descriptions, and manufacturer provenance.
Child Variants: Inherits parent attributes by default, while defining only differential properties (package size, dosage, channel price, barcode/EAN).

04 Channel-Level Attribute Management

A major feature I designed was Channel-Level Attribute Overrides. Netmeds, JioMart, and Milkbasket have differing storefront specifications (e.g. JioMart requires grocery-style pack dimensions, while Netmeds requires Schedule H drug warnings).

Rather than duplicating SKUs across databases, our system allowed brand managers to define channel-specific attribute mappings on top of the same underlying SKU hierarchy.

05 QA Test Coverage & Circular Dependency Prevention

To prevent catastrophic data corruption, I designed exhaustive edge-case test matrices covering:

  • Circular Variant Linking: Enforcing strict directed acyclic graph (DAG) checks to prevent SKU A referencing SKU B while SKU B references SKU A.
  • Unicode & Special Character Sanitization: Cleaning chemical compound formulas (e.g. β-blockers, ± dosages) to prevent frontend rendering crashes.
