The PM Craft & Execution Vault

Interactive PRD & Specs

Anatomy of how I structure end-to-end product delivery — from root-cause discovery to UX flows, technical schemas, QA sanity, and rollout telemetry.

Phase 1: Problem Statement & Root-Cause Signal

Why are we building this? (The User & Business Friction)

Commercial fleet supervisors manage between 500 and 3,000 active assets. During field shadowing, I observed operators spending an average of 4.2 minutes per alert cross-referencing three separate UI tabs (Vehicle Location, Fuel Sensor Logs, Driver Rosters) to diagnose basic operational issues like idling or fuel drops. This lag delayed urgent route and safety interventions.

// Explicit Non-Goals (Scope Containment to Prevent Creep)
• We are NOT replacing historical raw-data XLSX exports in Phase 1.
• We are NOT permitting automated vehicle engine immobilization without dual human confirmation.
• We are NOT introducing arbitrary SQL input fields that could expose production databases.
Phase 2: UX Discovery & Operator Workflow Mapping

Eliminating Multi-Tab Cognitive Friction

Operators don't need a conversational chatbot that chats; they need actionable vehicle intelligence with immediate operational controls. I mapped the user journey from question to action:

Step 1: Intent Query
Operator speaks or types: "Show idle reefers in North Zone".
Step 2: Structured Cards
System renders condensed vehicle status cards with live telemetry tags.
Step 3: Direct Actions
Embedded CTA buttons: [Call Driver], [Ping Telematics], [Assign Route].
Phase 3: Technical Feasibility & Architecture Alignment

De-Risking Delivery: Protecting User Experience & Infrastructure

A responsive user experience requires dependable infrastructure. Rather than handing engineering a vague wishlist, I leveraged my CS background to evaluate data volume alongside tech leads. To prevent sluggish queries and database degradation, we defined clear Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool contracts over in-memory Redis caching—guaranteeing sub-second UI response times for operators. 

POST /v2/ai/telemetry/query
{
  "operator_prompt": "Which reefer trucks in North Zone had temperature violations over 4°C today?",
  "tool_context": {
    "mcp_server": "roadcast-telemetry-mcp",
    "required_scope": ["fleet:read", "sensors:temperature"],
    "cache_fallback_ttl": 60
  }
}
Phase 4: QA Sanity & Edge-Case Guardrails

Hardening Against Real-World Operational Chaos

Offline & Network Dropouts
If vehicle GPS drops signal in transit tunnels, device buffers telemetry into local SQLite with SHA-256 idempotency key; auto-drains upon handshake reconnection.
Voice Safety Intercom Timeout
If an outbound driver safety call fails after 2 rings, trigger deterministic fallback SMS and escalate an immediate Priority-1 ticket to the 24/7 human operations control desk.
Engineering User Story & Gherkin Acceptance Criteria:
• Given a truck moving at >40 km/h with a Severity-1 DMS drowsiness alert,
• When the system triggers an outbound WebRTC call in Hindi,
• Then audio playback must start within <1,200ms; if no driver response in 8 seconds, transition state to [UNRESPONSIVE_ESCALATION].

Mummy Ki Rasoi: 16x Cloud Kitchen Scaling & Operations

Product Scope

Co-founded an affordable homestyle cloud kitchen during the pandemic. Reverse-engineered food aggregator ranking algorithms, rationalized menu SKUs via shared base preparations, transitioned to digital wholesale procurement (BigBasket & Hyperpure), and scaled volume 16x while protecting positive unit contribution margins.

Role: Co-founder & Head of Operations
Venture: Mummy Ki Rasoi
Duration: Jan 2020 – Jan 2021
Impact: 16x scale (5 → 80+ orders/day), 31% to 26% commission cut, profitable P&L

01 The 0-to-1 Baseline & Operational Bottlenecks

Launched on Swiggy and Zomato during COVID-19 with an initial volume of just ~5 orders/day. The business faced three acute operational bottlenecks: high platform take-rates (31% commission), unpredictable offline grocery procurement costs with high manual effort, and slow meal prep times (>25 min) causing delivery rider delays and algorithmic demotion.

02 Algorithmic Reverse-Engineering & Platform Discovery

Deconstructed Swiggy and Zomato suggestion rankings to understand recommendation scoring. Mapped the primary algorithm scoring levers: (a) sub-8 minute kitchen preparation time, (b) on-time rider handover avoiding delivery delays, (c) 100% menu availability during peak meal slots (12–3 PM, 7–10 PM), and (d) high packaging and customer rating averages.

03 Menu Engineering & SKU Rationalization

Redesigned the entire menu from scratch around shared preparation bases. Engineered modular recipes where multiple homestyle gravies and thali components shared base preparations. This cut raw ingredient SKU count drastically, eliminated perishable kitchen waste, reduced inventory holding costs, and accelerated ticket preparation time down to under 8 minutes.

04 Supply Chain Optimization & Customer Retention Flywheel

  • Digital Wholesale Procurement: Migrated from volatile offline local markets to digital wholesale procurement via BigBasket and Hyperpure, cutting input costs by 15-20% and saving 2+ daily operational hours.
  • Commission Renegotiation: Leveraged projected order volume trajectories and operational SLA metrics to renegotiate aggregator commissions from 31% down to 26%.
  • Customer Delight & Retention: Standardized portion sizes from real reviews; upgraded containers to leak-proof, microwave-safe packaging with zero unit-cost increase via direct distributor negotiations; and enclosed personalized handwritten customer notes in every order.

05 Scale, Unit Economics & Business Learnings

  • Scaled daily order volume 16x from 5 to 80+ orders/day in under 60 days.
  • Maintained consistent monthly profits documented in structured P&L balance sheets.
  • Built a 4.5+ star customer satisfaction rating with a high repeat-order cohort, validating how operational rigor and customer empathy drive unit economics.
Case Study Breakdown • Logistics Tech

Edgistify: Offline-First DAQ App & Warehousing Tech

Product Scope

Edgistify connects enterprises with flexible warehousing infrastructure across India. Ground operations teams conducted on-site physical audits of remote industrial warehouses to catalog facilities. In dead cellular zones, field audits frequently crashed or suffered data loss. Partnered with the product team to scope an offline-first data acquisition architecture.

Role: Product & Tech Intern
Company: Edgistify (IIT/NIT Alumni Startup)
Duration: Jun 2019 – May 2020
Impact: Offline-first DAQ app sync, Node.js web development, SEO keyword matrices

01 Field Operational Friction & Data Loss

Ground operations auditors visited remote industrial clusters and highway warehouses to record critical operational data—usable square footage, dock doors, ceiling heights, flooring specs, and multi-angle site photographs. Cellular reception in these remote industrial zones was frequently non-existent or erratic, causing form submission failures, image upload timeouts, and wasted field auditor trips.

02 Ground UX Discovery & Workflow Mapping

Shadowed ground audit teams to understand field constraints. Ground auditors were forced to swap between SIM cards, write audit notes on paper sheets, and manually re-enter data upon returning to hotel Wi-Fi. Recognized that the application must treat network unavailability as the default operational state rather than an exceptional error.

03 Offline-First Architecture & Idempotent Sync

Worked with the PM and Android developers on the DAQ (Data Acquisition) App:

  • Local Encrypted SQLite / Storage Cache: Structured multi-step forms and high-res site photos were written immediately to local device storage under unique audit UUIDs.
  • Network Broadcast Listener & Background Daemon: Designed an automatic background sync daemon. As soon as the device re-established any reliable network connection (cellular or Wi-Fi), the app securely queued, batch-compressed, and uploaded cached audits to the centralized database without requiring manual user intervention.
  • Zero Data Loss: Completely eliminated duplicate submissions via idempotency keys and provided clear local UI status badges (Saved Locally vs. Synced to Cloud).

04 Full-Stack Development & Cross-Functional Growth

  • Full-Stack Web Development: Developed web components using Node.js for Edgistify's core web platform, actively participating in daily Scrum meetings and sprint reviews.
  • Cross-Department Exposure: Explored Solutions Design, Operations, and Sales pipelines, collaborating directly with IIT/NIT alumni founders and understanding startup growth dynamics.

05 SEO Keyword Analytics & Measurable Outcomes

  • Built comprehensive keyword analysis matrices evaluating Keyword Difficulty (KD), Commercial Profitability, Search Volume, and Trend Trajectory.
  • Orchestrated high-authority directory submissions across logistics hubs and warehousing portals with structured tracking sheets, driving qualified inbound B2B organic traffic.
  • PM Takeaway: Validated that addressing low-level technical and environmental edge cases (like network dropouts) is fundamental to delivering resilient operational software.
